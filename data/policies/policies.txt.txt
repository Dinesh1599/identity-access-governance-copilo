Identity and Access Governance Policy

1. Department-based Access:
   - Only employees in the Finance department may access the finance resource.
   - HR documents are accessible only by employees in the HR department.

2. Role-based Access:
   - Approver role is required for approving payments.
   - Requestor role is required for submitting purchase requests.
   - Maker and Checker roles must never be assigned to the same user.

3. Segregation of Duties:
   - Users cannot simultaneously hold the roles of Maker and Checker.
   - Users cannot simultaneously hold the roles of Requestor and Approver.

4. General Principles:
   - All access is denied by default unless explicitly allowed.
   - Least privilege must be applied when granting new permissions.
   - Access reviews must occur quarterly.
